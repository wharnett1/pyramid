# pyramid
This is a prototype for pyramid, a health prioritization app

Disclaimer: I am not a medical professional. The goal of this project is not to provide medical advice, but to consolidate information from trusted doctors, scientists, and health professionals.

## Thesis:
The current state of social media and the internet has lead to widespread health misinformation and fearmongering. One of the ways this manifests is an overprioritization of highly specific, low-evidence, low-impact changes that influencers claim are necessary for everyone, while the average person doesn't have the basics down.

This app aims to combat this by assessing the user's health foundations through a brief survey to identify their strengths and weaknesses. We want to find the user's biggest unmet need and help them work on fixing it by providing the highest quality scientific evidence around that topic.

The core philosophy is to have solid foundations in the basic categories of health before working towards smaller-impact optimizations. In statistics, Pareto's principle states that that roughly 80% of results come from 20% of the causes. We aim to help the user get better at that 20% to get the a significant health and longevity impact without it consuming their life.

For advanced users who have solid foundations, we also want to provide the evidence for optimizations that are backed by science. We will show nuanced or incomplete information by adopting the internationally accepted GRADE system to be transparent about the quality of information.
